# cookpool-kitchen-calm — risks and open questions

- **Two toasts on Add item with a measure.** Adding the item and saving its measure each offer Undo. Acceptable for now; one combined toast is a follow-up.
- **Measures are per name and pantry** (SM-13). A measure added on one item shows on every item of that name in that pantry. That is the point, but worth knowing.
- **Who had it** leaves the item page as a card. History still names every person; if people miss the per-person totals, they come back inside History.
- **Use by choices** stay 3 days · 5 days · 2 wks · None · Date (the board drew 1 week · 1 month · 6 months). Open: whether to change them.
