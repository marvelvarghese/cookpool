## SE1 as built (SE-2)

- **Migration 530** (`530_spliteco_ledger`; 520 went to passkeys): `membership_organizations.made_in`, `kind`; `kitchen_txns.app`, `bucket`, `category`, `revises`, `moved_from`, `deleted_at`, `via_delegation`, `split_spec`; partial unique indexes on `revises` and `moved_from`; `ledger_txn_items`, `ledger_txn_item_shares`, `ledger_txn_adjustments`, `ledger_comments`.
- **Maths:** `packages/kitchen-engine/src/splits.ts` (six methods, itemized with portions/%/exact, tax/service/discount by items, several payers), on `splitByIntegerWeights`, the largest-remainder rule `splitByWeights` now delegates to.
- **API:** `apps/spliteco-worker` behind api-edge's `/v1/spliteco` facade (`SPLITECO_WORKER`), storage in `packages/db/src/spliteco`, wire in `packages/contracts/src/spliteco.ts`, client `client.spliteco` in `packages/sdk`. Grants `spliteco.ledger.read` (every group role) and `spliteco.expense.write` (all but viewer).
- **Groups:** membership-worker takes `madeIn`/`kind` on create, answers `?view=spliteco` (direct groups and members included) on the internal groups read, and finds or makes direct groups at `POST /v1/internal/membership/direct-groups`.
- **App:** `apps/spliteco-web` (Next.js on Workers, worker `cookpool-spliteco-web-<env>`), tests in `tests/spliteco-web`; worker tests and a local dev API in `tests/spliteco-worker`.

## Departures from the design

- **`kitchen_txns.title` was not added**: it exists since migration 220.
- **`split_spec` column added** (not in §1): the request an expense was saved from, so an edit can start from it.
- **A delete stamps `deleted_at` on its reversal row**, not on the original, so no row is ever updated; restore is a new version with `revises` pointing at the deleted one. `revises` and `moved_from` are unique when set.
- **Extra routes:** `GET /v1/spliteco/me/activity` (Activity across every group) and `POST /v1/spliteco/me/friends/{usr}/direct` (find or make the two-person group before the first expense outside groups; only for someone you share a group with, else 403 `no_shared_group`). Insights take `groupId` (not `org`) as the query name.
- **Direct groups are hidden from Cookpool now**, not only in SE3: membership-worker's public list and its default internal groups read leave out `kind = 'direct'`, and joining one by code reads as an unknown code. The public create refuses `kind: direct` and `direct-` slugs. They do not count toward the 50-group cap.
- **Two balances (§0.11):** everything added through spliteco-worker is bucket `other`, Cookpool's rows `kitchen`; a reversal keeps its original's bucket. The Kitchen balance equals kitchen-worker's balances for a group while it holds only Cookpool's rows. kitchen-worker is not changed in SE1 (outside SE-2's ceiling), so Cookpool still shows the whole ledger (Kitchen + Other) for a kitchen group that has Spliteco-added rows; its filter on `bucket = 'kitchen'` belongs to SE3. Tests pin both facts.
- **Home's "owed to you / you owe"** sum the per-person balances (as Splitwise reads), so they agree with the people listed; the net equals the sum of group balances.
- **Sign-in on the Spliteco host is the email code only.** Google needs the app's origin in identity-worker's `OAUTH_ALLOWED_CONSOLE_ORIGINS`, and passkeys need it in `PASSKEY_ORIGINS`/RP ID; identity-worker is outside SE-2's ceiling. The Google button is built behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_SPLITECO_GOOGLE=on`.
- **api-edge trusts the forwarded client address from the Spliteco app's binding** (`props.caller = "spliteco-web"`) as it does the console's.
- **Portions ×1000 make ⅓ a weight of 333**, so three people on ⅓ each still add up exactly (largest remainder), but ⅓ against 1 is 333:1000, not exactly 1:3.
- **Not in SE1, as planned:** settle up, reminders, scan, live split, assistants, the Cookpool-linked group screen; the app shows those entry points as disabled "Soon" or leaves them out.

Entry points from Home, Group and Friend.

## SE2 departures

- **No `spliteco.settlement.write` grant.** The policy engine (`packages/policy-engine`) is outside SE-3's `affects`, so payments, "not received", reminders and the simplify switch are gated on `spliteco.expense.write` — the same roles (every role but viewer). Adding the grant is a one-line change of `SETTLE_ACTION` in `apps/spliteco-worker/src/handlers/settle.ts` once policy-engine (and tests/policy-engine) are in a contract.
- **Simplify debts lives in its own table**, `spliteco_group_settings`, not on `kitchen_group_settings`: the first row there pins a group's currency, and switching simplify must not.
- **A payment that pays both balances is two rows** (Kitchen, then Other) sharing `source_kind = 'payment'` and `source_id`; the API answers one `payment` with its `parts`. "Not received" on either row reverses both.
- **What a payment may cover** is the group's plan between the two people in each balance (the fewest payments, or pair by pair with simplify off) — so with simplify on, paying someone the plan does not route you to is 409 `nothing_owed`.
- **Only the payer records a payment** (the caller is always `from`); the payee cannot record "they paid me cash". Settlements recorded in Cookpool are still Cookpool's (kitchen-worker), and its own reversal path is unchanged.
- **Reminders** go to someone who owes the caller by a shared group's plan (409 `nothing_owed` otherwise); without `groupId` the reminder is about the group they owe most in. `ledger_reminders` also keeps the amount and currency. A reminder is refused through a delegation only at the edge (SE5 plans a `send_reminder` tool); payments, "not received" and setting a UPI ID are also refused in the worker (403 `not_from_an_assistant`).
- **Push** rides on the email enqueue as for Cookpool's kinds, and is opt-in per kind; Cookpool's notification settings do not list the Spliteco kinds yet, so in practice these are emails until a push switch exists in an app. The push opens on the origin of the app that subscribed.
- **The reminder's 24-hour guard** is a read then a guarded insert; of two racing reminders only one is sent, but the loser's audit event is still written.
- **The UPI link** carries `pa`, `pn`, `am`, `cu=INR` and `tn` (the note, defaulting to the group's name); UPI is offered only for groups that count in INR.


## SE4 as built (SE-5)

SE4 was built before SE3: the cookpool-revamp epic is restyling Cookpool's Money screens, which SE3 rebuilds, so SE3 waits for it. SE4 needs only SE1's itemized model (sequencing note) and is stacked on SE2's branch.

- **Migration 550** (`550_spliteco_live_split`): `ledger_live_sessions` (lvs_ ids; org, an 8-character code from membership's join-code alphabet, unique; the draft as JSON — title, category, note, payers, items, adjustments; `open|locked`; created_by, created_at, expires_at 24 h on; the posted `txn_id`, `locked_at`), `ledger_live_claims` (session, item_position, subject_id, mode and value as an item share, updated_at) and `ledger_live_presence` (session, subject, seen_at). Storage in `packages/db/src/spliteco/live.ts`; `SplitecoRepository.write` takes further audit events and statements for the same batch.
- **API** (spliteco-worker, `handlers/live.ts`): `POST /v1/spliteco/groups/{org}/live` (needs `spliteco.expense.write`; the shares already set on Itemized carry over as claims; `spliteco.live.opened`), `GET /v1/spliteco/live/{code}` (the session, claims, who is here — read within 10 s — and the group's members; marks the caller here), `POST …/live/{code}/claims` (set or clear; your own, or anyone's for the opener — 403 `own_claims_only`), `POST …/live/{code}/lock` (opener only — 403 `not_the_opener`; 409 `not_all_claimed` with the unclaimed positions; posts one itemized expense through the expense path with `spliteco.expense.added` + `spliteco.live.locked` and the session's lock in one batch). After 24 h claims and the lock are 409 `live_expired` and the read says `expired`; a locked session answers claims with 409 `live_locked`. Contracts in `packages/contracts/src/spliteco.ts` (SE4 section), client `openLive`/`live`/`claimLive`/`lockLive`.
- **App:** `/scan` (take or choose a photo → shrunk to a 1200 px JPEG → `client.kitchen.aiReceipt`, the existing kitchen route through api-edge; "Read N items and M charges", "Did we read this right?" with Looks right / Fix it, editable charges and printed total; Assign items and Split the total equally stay off until every flagged line is checked and lines + charges equal the printed total; the draft goes to Add expense in sessionStorage and nothing is saved until Save; without a key, a budget or today's receipts it offers "Enter the items by hand"). "Scan a receipt" on Add expense. The "Live" button on Itemized opens `/live/{code}` (QR from `qrcode-generator` 1.4.4, MIT, no dependencies; code, Copy invite link, presence faces, claimed bar, item rows with claimers and "I had this", the opener's who-had-it sheet, the ticker, the lock). It polls every 2 s while visible and stops when hidden, locked or ended (`lib/poll.ts`). A signed-out `/live/{code}` link goes through Sign in and comes back to it.

## SE4 departures

- **A third table, `ledger_live_presence`.** Design §1 lists sessions only and the brief two tables; presence "by last poll" needs a write per poll, kept out of the claims so a poll never touches them.
- **Doubtful lines** are the reader's reading doubts only: no price, no name, reason `unclear`, or "not sure what …". Its other `check` reasons are about a kitchen's purchase history ("no price from before") and would flag almost every restaurant line, so they are not flagged.
- **Charges are recognised in the app.** The receipt route returns lines and a `totalMatches` flag, not the adjustments it summed. Lines whose text reads as tax, service or discount become charges; when the route says the total matches but lines and charges fall short, the gap is shown as one "Taxes and charges" (or "Discount") line the person can relabel. kitchen-worker is outside SE-5's contract, so the route was not changed.
- **PDFs are not read.** The route takes JPEG or WebP; a PDF asks for a screenshot instead. Any image the browser can draw is re-encoded to JPEG.
- **A claim is one portion by default**; the opener's sheet sets one portion per person. Portions, % and exact claims are accepted by the API (and checked by the engine at the lock), but the Live screen offers only "I had this"; finer shares are set on Itemized before going live.
- **The opener must still hold `spliteco.expense.write` at the lock**, and everyone named must still be a member (422 `not_in_group`). Reading and claiming need only membership (viewers may claim).
- **Polling uses the edge's Spliteco budget** (60 reads a minute per person); a 2-second poll is 30. Not changed (risk SE-E).
- **Sign-in returns to where it was asked for** (`lib/return-to.ts`, sessionStorage, own-origin paths only), for every page, not only live links.
- **The dev API** (`tests/spliteco-worker/src/dev-server.ts`) stubs the receipt route with the canvas's Beach Shack bill (`SPLITECO_DEV_RECEIPT=unavailable` answers as without a key).


## SE5 as built (SE-6)

SE5 was built stacked on SE4's branch. MC-4 (the consent page) is on main, so SE5 did not wait.

- **Scope `money.write`** (`packages/contracts/src/delegations.ts`, `mcp-connect.ts`; consent page row "Add and edit Spliteco expenses · Never records payments", off by default — one entry in `apps/web-console-next/src/components/kitchen/assistants.ts`, nothing restyled). api-edge's allowlist (`delegation-scopes.ts`) opens, for money.read, `GET` home, groups, activity (yours and a group's), search, a friend, a group, an expense and insights; for money.write, `POST /drafts`, `POST /drafts/{drf}/save`, `DELETE /expenses/{txn}`, `POST …/restore`, `POST …/comments` and `POST /reminders`. Everything else under `/v1/spliteco` stays refused to every delegated token: settlements, not-received, the profile (UPI ID), the settle plan, group create and settings, live split, export, the direct-group route — and the add, edit and move routes themselves. spliteco-worker checks the same list again by route name (`src/delegated.ts`): 403 `not_from_an_assistant` for payments, `draft_required` for a straight-away add/edit/move, `scope` for the rest. The edge forwards the assistant's name (`x-actor-delegation-client`).
- **Migration 560** (`560_spliteco_assistants`, kitchen): `kitchen_txns.via_client` (the assistant's name beside `via_delegation`) and `ledger_drafts` (drf_ ids; person, delegation, add|edit|move, group, expense, the request as JSON, 10 minutes, saved_txn_id). **Migration 570** (`570_identity_delegation_access`, identity): `identity_delegations.granted_scopes`, the scopes allowed at connect.
- **Drafts** (`handlers/drafts.ts`): `POST /v1/spliteco/drafts` checks everything the write would (membership and role, the split adding up, people in the group, currency, Cookpool's rows, the current version, members of the target group for a move) and answers the split per person with names, before and after for an edit or move, and the token — saving nothing. `POST /drafts/{drf}/save` runs the ordinary create/edit/move handler with the token as its Idempotency-Key, so a second save answers the same expense. A draft is the person's and the delegation's that made it (anyone else's, or the person's own session, reads 404); after 10 minutes it is 409 `draft_expired`. Kept in D1, not KV: D1 is already bound to spliteco-worker, the draft must be read in the same place the write happens, and a KV namespace would need `infra/terraform` and new wiring, which SE-6's contract does not reach.
- **Search:** `GET /v1/spliteco/me/search?q&groupId&limit` (every word in the title or note, current rows, newest first).
- **Activity:** entries and expense summaries carry `viaAssistant` (the name, or "an assistant" for an assistant row without one); the app's Activity chip reads "via Claude", and the expense page says it after "added by".
- **mcp-worker** (`src/tools/spliteco.ts`): find_people, list_groups, get_balances, search_expenses, get_activity, get_insights and settle_up (money.read); draft_expense, edit_expense, move_expense, save_expense, delete_expense, restore_expense, add_comment and send_reminder (money.write). Every split method and several payers, in the API's own shape (minor units). Each tool has plain-words refusals (`explain`). settle_up calls nothing and answers `https://cookpool-spliteco-web-<env>.cookpool.workers.dev/settle?group=&to=` (or `/friend?id=`). `SPLITECO_ORIGIN` (wrangler template) is that host; it is also the one other origin `/connect/disconnect` answers. The server's instructions say to show a draft and save only on yes.
- **Look only / Add & edit:** identity's new `PATCH /v1/auth/delegations/{dlg}` `{ scopes }` (session only; within `grantedScopes`; security event `delegation.updated`), routed by api-edge's auth facade, `client.auth.updateDelegation` in the SDK.
- **spliteco-web:** You → AI assistants (`/you/assistants`, `lib/assistants.ts`): the MCP address from the host (`cookpool-mcp-worker-<env>.cookpool.workers.dev/mcp`, localhost in dev), Copy, each connected assistant with its last use, Look only · Add & edit and Disconnect (identity revoke, then mcp-worker's disconnect, best effort), and the canvas's "Assistants can" and "Only in this app" lists. The dev API (`tests/spliteco-worker/src/dev-server.ts`) answers made-up delegations for clicking through.
- **Tests:** an end-to-end walk of the canvas's AssistantChat in `tests/spliteco-worker/src/assistant-e2e.test.ts` (mcp-worker's real tools → real api-edge → real spliteco-worker over SQLite; identity faked).

## SE5 departures

- **People on an expense an assistant changed are not notified.** No expense change notifies anyone today (SE1 built none; `spliteco.expense_for_you` in §5 was never made), so there is no notify path to reuse, and a new kind needs a template in notifications-worker, which is outside SE-6's contract. An assistant's change shows in Activity "via Claude" like any edit; the canvas line "Anu, Dev and Meera were told" is not built, and the AI assistants page does not claim it. Follow-up: a task with notifications-worker in its contract adds `spliteco.expense_for_you` and spliteco-worker sends it on every add, edit, delete, restore and move (an assistant's included).
- **An assistant adds, edits and moves only through drafts.** The brief allowlisted the expense create, edit and move routes for money.write; they are allowlisted as `POST /drafts` and `…/save` instead, and the routes themselves are refused to delegated tokens at the edge and in the worker, so nothing can be saved that was not drafted. Delete and restore are direct (soft, restorable for 30 days).
- **Look only / Add & edit can only narrow, then widen back, within what was allowed at connect.** The assistant's OAuth grant carries the scopes allowed at connect and the provider cannot widen them on refresh, so "Add & edit" for an assistant connected without money.write says to reconnect instead. After "Look only" the assistant still lists its write tools until it reconnects; each call is refused (403 `scope`, "change it in Spliteco under You → AI assistants") within the edge's 30-second actor cache.
- **money.write does not include money.read.** The scopes are independent, as the console's switches are: drafting needs ids, so an assistant without money.read can draft only with ids it was given. The consent page shows both switches off by default.
- **restore_expense is an extra tool**, beside the listed ones, so "deleted ones come back" works from the assistant too.
- **Comments and reminders record no delegation** (`ledger_comments`, `ledger_reminders` have no `via_delegation`); only ledger rows do. A reminder from an assistant reads as from the person.
- **Disconnect from Spliteco also ends the MCP grant** only where mcp-worker's `SPLITECO_ORIGIN` matches the app's origin (stage and prod are set); elsewhere it revokes the delegation at identity, which already stops every tool.
