## Round two — Money stays in Cookpool

The owner asked for every Money feature inside Cookpool, with no trip to the
Spliteco app. Spliteco's own screens are mounted in Cookpool rather than
copied:

| Task | What | State | PR |

| MN-8 | Cookpool mounts group, Add expense, expense, Settle up and Scan under /money; the tab stops handing off | shipped | this PR |

### How MN-8 mounts them

- Cookpool depends on `@saas/spliteco-web` and renders its pages under /money: group, add, expense, settle-up (Spliteco's /settle; /money/settle stays the kitchen's), scan, friend, move, share, insights.
- `SplitecoScreen` gives them Cookpool's signed-in client through Spliteco's `SessionContext`, maps their links (`splitecoToCookpool`) and draws them in Spliteco's stylesheet scoped under `.spx` (generated by scripts/spliteco-inside-css.mjs; a test fails when it is stale). Sheets portal into that wrapper.
- Spliteco's Home, You, Activity and Live are not mounted; a link to them returns to the Money tab.
- **Deploys:** a change to apps/spliteco-web alone does not redeploy Cookpool (orun plans by component). Touch web-console-next in the same PR when a Spliteco screen changes.
