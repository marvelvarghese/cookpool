# money-inside — risks and open questions

- **Cookpool's old Money screens** (`MoneyScreen`, Bill, the kitchen-only Settle up) are no longer on `/money`; they stay in the code for now. Removing them is a follow-up.
- **Whole balance, not Kitchen only.** Cookpool's Money used to show the Kitchen part; Spliteco inside shows everything you owe or get back. That is the point, but people used to the old figure may notice.
- **Leaving the app to settle.** Settle up opens Spliteco in the same tab; Spliteco's "← Back" returns. Settling inside the tab is a later step.
- **Not seen signed in on stage** until the hosts ship.
